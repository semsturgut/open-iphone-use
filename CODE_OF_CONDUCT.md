# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique behavior and code with reproducible evidence, not personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation and publishing another person's private information are not welcome.

Keep reports focused on authorized devices/accounts and remove private text or credentials from examples. Repository maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect contributors. Report conduct issues privately to the repository owner through their public profile; agree on a private channel before sending sensitive details.
