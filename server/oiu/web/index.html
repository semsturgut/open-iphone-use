Your phones, together.

Connecting

USB phones

Connect an iPhone on iOS 27 by cable, trust this Mac and turn on Developer Mode (Settings → Privacy & Security). Xcode 27 must be installed and opened once with the phone connected.

Bluetooth phones (legacy)

Bluetooth HID is required. Pair each iPhone in AssistiveTouch → Devices → Bluetooth Devices → Open iPhone Use, then add that phone below.

USB phones stream live; click the screen to tap. Legacy Bluetooth phones need their QR code scanned on the iPhone and Safari screen sharing approved.
